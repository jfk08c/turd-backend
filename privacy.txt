Privacy Policy for Turd Hunt Twitch Extension

**Last Updated:** May 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ItsKerbs ("we", "us", or "our") handles user data for the Turd Hunt Twitch Extension (the "Extension"). We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing a fun, interactive streaming experience.

1. Information We Collect
To provide the interactive features of this Extension, we utilize the following information:
* **Twitch User ID and Username:** If you explicitly grant permission by opting into "Identity Linking" within the Extension, we collect your unique Twitch User ID and display name.
* **Extension Interaction Data:** We collect real-time interaction data (such as button clicks, game inputs, or custom commands) to process game logic and display interactions on the stream overlay.

2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected data strictly to operate the Extension's core features, which include:
* Linking your interactions to your Twitch profile for leaderboard tracking, game state persistence, or stream interaction.
* Transmitting real-time events to the broadcaster's stream overlay via secure WebSocket connections.

3. Data Storage and Retention
* Any data required for the functionality of the extension is processed securely through our backend services hosted on Render.
* We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. 
* Data is only retained for as long as necessary to maintain the extension's interactive features (e.g., active session data or leaderboard standings).

4. Third-Party Services
This Extension operates within the Twitch platform. Twitch may collect analytics and data independently under their own Privacy Policy. We do not integrate separate third-party analytics trackers (such as Google Analytics) that track you across the web.

5. Your Choices and Data Deletion
You can revoke Identity Linking permissions at any time through your Twitch account settings. 6. Updates to This Policy
